# A companion beside the book

Open the sparkle button while reading, or choose **Reading companion** on a
book's details page. Ask about the current text, or open **People**, **Places**
and **Timeline** to build a reading guide.

The default range ends at the passage you were reading. The menu also offers
the whole chapter, with a warning that it includes later passages. Long chapters
use a bounded selection; the guide shows which passages it covers. A preface
can itself discuss later events, so a passage boundary is not a guarantee that
the original text contains no spoilers.

Each guide entry includes a short quotation and a **Read passage** button.
People appear in an interactive graph or tree layout. Select a person to focus
on their connections, or choose **Show all** for the complete returned graph.
Names remain available in the searchable index. Drag, pinch, use the zoom
controls, or expand the chart. A tree layout does not by itself imply ancestry.

**Places** offers a geographic map and a story map. The offline atlas supplies
real coastlines and city coordinates. A uniquely matching name appears as a
suggested pin; select it to confirm or change the location. Ambiguous names and
fictional places remain unplaced until a location is chosen. Choices stay on the
device. Map lines represent connections in the text, rather than roads or
historical boundaries. The story map remains available for fictional worlds.

**Timeline** switches between narrative order and a proportional date axis.
Only time phrases found in the quoted source can become calendar dates. Unknown,
relative and approximate dates keep their narrative labels. Events sharing a
date remain separately selectable. Select an event to read its details and proof.

Guides cover the displayed passages. A whole-book overview uses selected chapter
excerpts and shows those chapters and partial coverage; it is not an exhaustive
index of the book. Illustrations generated by AgInTi are not yet enabled.

Guides can make mistakes. A matching quotation verifies where the quoted words
occur, not whether every interpretation is correct. Check important details
against the linked passage and use **Report response** when needed.

## Reuse and privacy

Source-based guides are reused for the same public edition, language and passage
range. Simultaneous requests share one generation. Cached guides do not consume
another answer allowance; new generation and personal questions use the existing
companion allowance.

Saved guides remain readable offline. On reconnect, Bunko checks for a current
guide and removes an obsolete cached edition. Personal questions and recent
conversation are isolated by account, book and reading range. They never enter
the shared guide cache. Reading progress, books, dictionaries and offline reading
remain available independently of optional Reading plans.

The companion asks for consent before sending a question or generating a guide
with DeepSeek. Reading plans use the Reader, Creator and Entrepreneur names.
Availability and purchase controls continue to follow the qualified store
catalogue for each platform.

## Map data

The bundled atlas uses Natural Earth's 1:50m land outlines and 1:10m populated
places, including multilingual city names. It loads with the map and remains
available offline in the installed reader. No location query is sent to a
geocoding service.

Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
The city-name source includes CC0 Wikidata names. Exact source revisions and
hashes are in [the atlas manifest](reading-atlas-provenance.json); the converter
is `tools/build-reading-atlas.py`. This atlas is a reading reference, not a
street-navigation map.
