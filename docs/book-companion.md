# A companion beside the book

Open the sparkle button while reading, or choose **Reading companion** on a
book's details page. Ask about the current text, or open **People**, **Places**
and **Timeline** to build a reading guide.

The default range ends at the passage you were reading. The menu also offers
the whole chapter, with a warning that it includes later passages. Long chapters
use a bounded selection; the guide shows which passages it covers. A preface
can itself discuss later events, so a passage boundary is not a guarantee that
the original text contains no spoilers.

Each guide entry includes a short quotation and a **Read passage** button.
People can have aliases and relationships. Place diagrams show connections
described by the text; they are schematic, without invented distances or
coordinates. The timeline follows the order of events in the text and shows a
date only when one is supplied. Interpretations are labelled.

Guides can make mistakes. A matching quotation verifies where the quoted words
occur, not whether every interpretation is correct. Check important details
against the linked passage and use **Report response** when needed.

## Reuse and privacy

Source-based guides are reused for the same public edition, language and passage
range. Simultaneous requests share one generation. Cached guides do not consume
another answer allowance; new generation and personal questions use the existing
companion allowance.

Saved guides remain readable offline. On reconnect, Bunko checks for a current
guide and removes an obsolete cached edition. Personal questions and recent
conversation are isolated by account, book and reading range. They never enter
the shared guide cache. Reading progress, books, dictionaries and offline reading
remain available independently of optional Reading plans.

The companion asks for consent before sending a question or generating a guide
with DeepSeek. Reading plans use the Reader, Creator and Entrepreneur names.
Availability and purchase controls continue to follow the qualified store
catalogue for each platform.
