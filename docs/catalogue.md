# Catalogue: per-title rights audit

Updated 2026-09-23. **A title ships only if its row here says `ship`.** `tools/build_reader.py --all-cleared` reads exactly this file and refuses anything else, so the audit is the gate, not a note beside it.

Two questions are asked of every title. Is the original out of copyright in the United States, the European Union and Japan? And is every text the reader will see either out of copyright too, or generated by this pipeline from the public-domain original, which makes it LazyingArt's own work?

The second question is the one that bites. Each assembled book in `../ZhJpBook/data/interlinear/<slug>/assembled/` records the sources it was built from. Where those sources are Wikisource, Project Gutenberg, Aozora Bunko or the curated public-domain canon, the gloss is a clean derivative of a public-domain text. Where a modern commercial edition appears among them, a scholarly translation someone else owns may have shaped the output, and no amount of good intent makes that shippable. Those titles are held until the pipeline re-runs them from public-domain sources alone.

Counts: **56 ship**, 41 held for a clean re-run, 19 with no data yet. 金瓶梅, 肉蒲團 and 素女經 are excluded on age rating whatever their copyright status.
