## Changes

The reading companion is unavailable for the mainland China storefront, device
region or network. An unresolved Apple storefront keeps it unavailable until
confirmed. The app hides companion entrances and shows a short Settings notice;
the server independently rejects generation, uploads, imports and resume actions.
Books, offline reading, dictionaries, discussions and account access remain usable.
Existing reading positions, downloaded books and conversations are preserved.

Nearby book figures now load when scrolling even if WebKit delays its visibility
observer. The cumulative historical-map corrections from build38 are included.

The live reading service starts with DeepSeek Flash and can use Pro for the
existing bounded repair attempt or provider fallback. Administrator settings
provide Default and For harder questions. See [model routing](reading-models-20261025.md).
