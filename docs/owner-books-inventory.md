# Owner bookshelf cross-check · 2026-09-25

Compared the public [LazyLearn](https://learn.lazying.art/) and [LazyEarn](https://earn.lazying.art/) shelves with their adjacent, read-only source checkouts. Bunko ships reviewed JSON bundles from [bunko-books](https://github.com/lachlanchen/bunko-books); the app repository contains no book payload.

## LazyLearn and physics

- All **10 core** Leonard Susskind course-note editions are represented: six Theoretical Minimum volumes and the four other public core runs (Classical Mechanics Stanford partial, Quantum Mechanics Stanford, General Relativity 2008, Cosmology legacy).
- All **9 supplementary** course-note editions are represented: Advanced Quantum Mechanics; Particle Physics 1–3; Quantum Entanglement Parts 1 and 3; Topics in String Theory: Cosmology and Black Holes; String Theory and M-Theory; and Demystifying the Higgs Boson.
- The two featured LazyLearn books are represented: *How to Speak and Write* and *Justice with Michael Sandel*. These are currently English companion editions in Bunko. The former also has Chinese and Japanese PDFs in LazyLearn; their paragraph alignment still needs editorial review before adding those layers to the Bunko edition.

Physics content is independent, AI-assisted companion notes, **not** Susskind-authored or endorsed. Source links and GPL-3.0 terms are in each book's rights record. Equations remain TeX for local KaTeX rendering and source figures are compressed for the mobile reader.
