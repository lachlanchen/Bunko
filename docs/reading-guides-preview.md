# A reading companion with places, people and dates

Bunko's next test update helps you follow a book without losing your place.
Open the companion from the reader to carry the current book and passage with
you. Add compact @ references to a book, chapter, paragraph or selected sentence.
Follow a reference back to the exact passage, then return to the conversation.

## People and relationships

Explore a graph or tree, search for a name, focus on one person's connections,
or expand the overview. Relationship labels and source passages stay accessible.
A tree layout is a way to arrange the graph; it does not imply a family tree
unless the text supports those relationships.

![Character relationships in the test preview](images/reading-guides-20261021/phone-graph-focused.png)

## Places on a map

The geographic view uses an offline atlas. Pan and zoom, open a place's source
passage, or confirm and adjust its suggested pin. Ambiguous and fictional places
remain unplaced until a reader chooses a location; the story graph remains
available. Pin choices stay local, and opening the map sends no geocoding query.

![Map with a labelled demonstration journey](images/reading-guides-20261021/phone-map.png)

## Events over time

Switch between narrative order and a calendar timeline. Calendar placement uses
date phrases in cited passages, preserving their year, month or day precision.
Simultaneous events have separate lanes; unknown and approximate dates remain
separate. The result is a reading aid, not a historical-calendar authority.

![Timeline with labelled demonstration events](images/reading-guides-20261021/phone-timeline.png)

## Preview scope

These are screenshots of real reader components in a browser test fixture,
captured on 21 October 2026 Hong Kong time. The relationship example comes from
the public *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* preface with source-checked quotations.
The map and timeline use an explicitly labelled fictional demonstration journey.
They are not photographs of an installed native app or current store screenshots.

Guides cover the chosen excerpts or a bounded sample of a book. They may omit
characters or events; consult the displayed coverage and supporting text.
Model-generated explanations can contain errors. AI-generated map illustrations
and additional sign-in providers are not enabled by this update.

The implementation is in [the visualization update](https://github.com/lachlanchen/Bunko/commit/dce76a2760b4a254b372fa32c1bb1736e1c979d8).
This preview does not announce a public release. Store editions and internal
tests may have different features; check the notes for your installed build.
