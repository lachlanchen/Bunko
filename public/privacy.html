Privacy

Bunko · LazyingArt LLC · updated 23 September 2026

Bunko collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting, and no server of ours that your reading passes through. We could not tell you what anyone reads, because we never receive it.

What stays on your device

Which books you downloaded, where you stopped reading in each of them, and your settings: languages shown, arrangement, readings, text size and theme. All of it is written to your device's local storage by the app itself. Deleting the app deletes it. Nothing is synchronised anywhere.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Only requests for the books themselves. When you open the library or download a book, the app fetches JSON files from the public GitHub repository lachlanchen/bunko-books, through GitHub's own servers and the jsDelivr content network. Those services see an ordinary web request: an IP address, a time, and which file was asked for. They are not operated by us and we receive nothing from them. Their handling is described by GitHub and jsDelivr.

If you use “Request a book”, the app opens GitHub's issue form in your browser with the title you typed. Nothing is sent until you choose to submit it there, and what you submit is public.

Children

Bunko is a reading app for works of literature. It has no social features, no user-generated content, no advertising and no purchases inside it. Because it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.
