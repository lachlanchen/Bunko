Cloud service terms

Optional cloud subscriptions are being prepared. A planned price or a disabled button is not an offer to charge you. A subscription starts only after you explicitly confirm the available plan in the payment provider's checkout.

Your reader remains yours

The Bunko app purchase includes access to the downloadable library, dictionaries and offline reading. Cloud plans cover new PDF conversion and AI companion usage. Canceling a cloud plan does not remove downloaded books or local notes. Deleting the app can remove local data.

Monthly cloud plans

The planned monthly US prices are Reader $2.99 (200 PDF pages, 40 AI answers daily), Researcher $14.99 (1,200 pages, 80 answers daily), and Studio $29.99 (2,600 pages, 160 answers daily). The purchase screen shows the actual currency, price, billing period and any applicable tax before confirmation. Page allowances reset each subscription period; AI allowances reset each UTC day. Unused allowances do not roll over and have no cash value.

Eligible subscribers may receive a seven-day trial with 50 PDF pages and 10 AI answers daily. The store determines eligibility; any trial and the subsequent monthly price are shown before purchase. A trial automatically becomes a paid monthly subscription unless canceled before its end.

Renewal, restoration and cancellation

Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Use Cloud plans → Manage subscription or your original Apple, Google Play or Stripe subscription settings to cancel future renewal. Canceling normally retains access until the current paid period ends. Refund and revocation decisions follow the payment provider and applicable law. Removing the app, signing out or deleting a document does not cancel a subscription.

Use the same Bunko account and store account to restore purchases. Plans currently apply only to Bunko. Shared subscriptions or discounts across LazyingArt apps have not been introduced.

Documents, allowances and availability

Upload only material you have permission to process. Your companion documents are private. AI answers can be inaccurate; check the original sources. Existing converted documents do not use new PDF conversion allowance simply by being opened. Failed requests release unused reservations; an uncertain provider submission may keep a reservation while its result is checked so it cannot be submitted or charged twice. File-size limits and shared service capacity still apply.

Cloud processing requires an Internet connection and available providers. Usage and renewal information in the app comes from verified provider records. If a payment completes but access does not update, restore purchases or contact support; avoid purchasing again.
